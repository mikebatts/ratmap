# Brand & Voice

## Tone

NYC-direct. Slightly cheeky. Respects the city. Doesn't fearmonger. Doesn't
shame. The product is a *tool*, not a meme.

## Tagline

In use: **"NYC's rat map. No fluff."**

Alternatives kept on file: "Where the rats at." · "Know before you walk." ·
"Rats. We have data."

## Visual

- **Accent:** hot dog yellow `#F5C518` — the universal NYC street-food signal.
- **Body:** dark ink `#1A1A1A` on cream `#FAF7F0`.
- **Type:** Inter for body, Space Grotesk for display/headers.
- **Pins:** small, clean, category-colored.
- No emoji. No cartoon rats.

## Copy direction

**Do**

- "We pulled every 311 rat complaint and DOH inspection. Free, no account, no ads."
- "Your block's rat report card."
- "Updated daily from the city's own data."

**Don't**

- "OMG NYC RATS ARE EVERYWHERE"
- "The ickiest map on the internet"
- Anything that uses fear or shame.
